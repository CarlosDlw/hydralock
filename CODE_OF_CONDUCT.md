# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- being respectful and constructive;
- accepting feedback gracefully;
- focusing on technical merit and project quality.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- harassment, insults, or discriminatory language;
- bad-faith disruption of discussions or reviews;
- publishing private information without consent.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and private communications related to project collaboration.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to project maintainers.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
